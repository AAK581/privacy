# Privacy Policy - From Boorhees, With Love

Last updated: August 22, 2026

From Boorhees, With Love ("the game") is developed by Doma (doma581). This policy
explains what data the game handles.

## Data the developer collects

None. The game has no accounts, no login, no analytics of my own, and no
server of my own. Your save files stay on your device.

## Data collected by Google

The game shows **no ads**. It offers an optional, voluntary "Support the
dev!" tip purchase through **Google Play Billing**. Purchases are processed
entirely by Google Play over an encrypted (TLS) connection; I never see or
store your payment details.

How Google uses data is described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

The game is not directed at children and is not designed for users under 13.

## Deleting your data

I hold no data about you, so there is nothing for me to delete. Uninstalling
the game removes your local save files. To remove data held by Google, use
your [Google account controls](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy).
